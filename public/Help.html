Help Center

Everything you need to know about booking your seat with PolyCoach.

1. Register

Click Register on the Home Page and fill in your full name and phone number, then choose your bus. Depending on the current trip, you will also select your destination or origin. Your departure venue is filled in automatically.

Once submitted you are ready to proceed to booking.

Already registered before? Go straight to Book — no need to register again.

2. Start Your Booking

Click Book on the Home Page. Enter your phone number to look up your registration. You will then be asked whether you are booking for yourself or for someone else.

If you are booking for yourself, your details are used directly. If you are booking for someone else, enter their full name, phone number, destination or origin, and choose their bus.

You must be registered before you can book. If you have not registered yet, click Register first.

3. Send Your Payment

On the payment page you will see our payment details — National Bank, Airtel Money, and TNM Mpamba. Send a deposit starting from the minimum amount shown to secure your seat. The amount you send is part of your transport fare, not an additional charge.

Take a clear screenshot of your mobile money confirmation and upload it on the same page. You cannot proceed to seat selection without uploading your proof of payment.

Make sure your screenshot clearly shows the transaction amount, recipient, and reference. Blurry or incomplete screenshots will delay your verification.

4. Choose Your Seat

After uploading your payment proof you will see the full bus layout. Green seats are available — click any green seat to select it, then press Confirm Booking.

Your seat will immediately show as pending on the bus layout while your payment is being reviewed by an administrator. No one else can take it during this time.

Already booked before under the same name? The system will detect this and give you options — you can switch seats, keep your existing seat, or continue as a different passenger.

5. Verification and Your Ticket

Once an administrator reviews and approves your payment, your seat status changes from pending to booked. You will receive a receipt with your seat number, passenger details, and a QR code.

The QR code can be scanned on the day of travel to verify your booking instantly. Keep it accessible — on your phone is fine.

If your seat has not been confirmed within a reasonable time after uploading your proof, contact us using the details below.

6. View Bus Status

You can check the live seat layout at any time by clicking View Bus Status on the Home Page. No login is required — it is open to everyone.

When the page opens, select which bus you want to view. You will see all 72 seats colour-coded — green for available, yellow for pending, and red for booked. Hover over any seat to see the name of the passenger who has reserved it.
